Privacy Policy for 2048 Classic

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

2048 Classic is a mobile puzzle game developed by Godwin Metri ("I", "me", or "the developer"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use 2048 Classic. The game stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device and uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and interstitial advertisements.

2. Information Stored by 2048 Classic

2048 Classic uses local storage to retain gameplay information, which may include your current board and score, best score, saved-game state, Undo information, and sound preferences.

This information supports gameplay, saved-game recovery, and remembering your preferences. The game's local-storage functionality is not designed to upload this information to the developer's servers.

2048 Classic does not require an account or ask you to provide personal information to play. If you email the developer, information you include in your message will be used to respond to your inquiry.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

2048 Classic uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, and applicable services, information processed may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Device or advertising identifiers, where applicable.

The information collected and how it is used may depend on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized where applicable.

The developer does not directly collect this advertising information through the game's own local gameplay storage. Google Mobile Ads may independently collect and process information to provide its services.

For further information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising technologies
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the game or its advertising provider may be used to:

  • Save and restore gameplay progress.
  • Remember game preferences, including sound settings.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Support advertising measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.
  • Respond to messages sent to the developer.

5. Data Sharing

The game's local gameplay and storage functionality is not designed to transmit saved game progress or sound preferences to the developer. Google Mobile Ads may collect or share information as necessary to provide advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud-prevention services, subject to Google's applicable practices.

Information you voluntarily send to the developer may be used to respond to your inquiry. The developer does not sell locally stored gameplay progress or preferences.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information and preferences remain on your device until they are overwritten, cleared, or removed with the app's data. You can generally delete them by clearing 2048 Classic's app storage or uninstalling the game. This may permanently erase saved progress.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. Information received through email may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain relevant correspondence.

7. Children's Privacy

2048 Classic does not require players to create an account or submit personal information to play. Its intended audience and advertising configuration must comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families policies if the game is directed toward children or is likely to attract a child audience.

If you believe information has been handled inappropriately, please contact the developer.

8. Security

The game uses local storage for gameplay information and preferences rather than a developer-operated account database. Information handled by third-party services is subject to their security practices. Google documents encryption in transit for information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You can stop using 2048 Classic at any time, clear its local storage or uninstall it, and manage available advertising or privacy settings through your Android device and Google's controls. Available controls may vary by device and region.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features, advertising integrations, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be available at the public privacy-policy URL associated with 2048 Classic.
